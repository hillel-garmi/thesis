  \begin{abstract}
    We calculate the Galois group of $f = x^n + ax^k + b \in \mathbb{Z}\left[ x \right]$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ for all $1 \leq \left| ab \right| \leq 3$, $n$ sufficiently large and $k$ coprime to $n$.
    In particular, we prove that the Galois group of $f$ is the maximal one that its factorization type allows.
    We do it by proving a new sufficient condition for the Galois group of a factor of a trinomial to be symmetric, which perhaps can be used to derive stronger results of this kind in the future.
  \end{abstract}
